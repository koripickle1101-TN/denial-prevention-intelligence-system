Dataset Purpose

This synthetic dataset is a learning tool for practicing review-priority scoring, claim-readiness labels, root-cause hypotheses, next-action documentation, patient-access risk awareness, and modeled revenue-risk levels.

Portfolio Use

The sample cases let me practice asking what should be reviewed, who might own the next action, and what evidence would be needed before a fictional case moves downstream.

Scope Boundary

The case labels, scores, and next actions are student-designed assumptions. They do not establish coding accuracy, medical necessity, coverage, payer compliance, reimbursement, or real denial risk.
