ControlWhat It Practices
Denial Traceback Review™Tracing a visible signal backward to the earliest supported condition.

Student Interpretation

DPIS now treats denial categories and repeated corrections as signals for investigation rather than automatic root-cause conclusions. I use the dashboard to practice identifying where a problem became visible, what earlier checkpoint is supported for review, who owns the next action, what would verify closure, and whether a similar pattern appears again.
