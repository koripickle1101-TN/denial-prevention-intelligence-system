Claim Readiness Review Checklist

A student-developed checklist for practicing how unresolved administrative review flags might be surfaced before a fictional case moves downstream. It is not a production claim-submission checklist.

Simulated Readiness Questions

Review AreaQuestion
EligibilityHas the fictional coverage information been reviewed for the modeled date of service?
AuthorizationHave authorization requirements and service/date alignment been reviewed in the simulation?
DocumentationIs any documentation-readiness flag identified and routed for appropriate qualified review?
CodingHas any coding-related review flag been routed to an appropriately qualified coding resource rather than independently resolved in this student model?
DemographicsHave fictional member identifiers and demographic fields been checked for obvious inconsistencies?
Payer RequirementsHas the relevant current payer requirement been identified for review by the appropriate role?
Final Workflow GateDoes the fictional case have any unresolved administrative flag that should prevent it from moving to the next modeled step?

Student Control Question

Before the simulated case moves downstream, what remains unresolved, who owns the next action, and what evidence would confirm closure?

Scope Boundary

This checklist does not determine coding accuracy, medical necessity, coverage, compliance, payment, or payer policy. It is an educational workflow-review artifact only.
