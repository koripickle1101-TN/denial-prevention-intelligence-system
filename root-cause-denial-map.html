Denial Signal Root-Cause Review Map

A student-developed map for practicing how a downstream denial or rejection signal might prompt review of earlier workflow conditions. The categories are hypotheses for investigation, not confirmed causes.

Simulated Review Categories

Downstream SignalEarlier Condition to ReviewStudent Control Question
Eligibility-related signalCoverage information or member data may be inconsistentWas eligibility information reviewed and any mismatch resolved?
Authorization-related signalAuthorization details may not align with the planned service or timingWas authorization-service alignment reviewed before the next step?
Documentation-related signalRequired supporting documentation may appear incomplete in the fictional caseWas the documentation flag routed for appropriate qualified review?
Coding-related signalA coding-related discrepancy may require qualified coding reviewWas the issue routed to an appropriately qualified coding resource rather than assumed resolved?
Timeliness-related signalAging, queue delay, or repeated rework may be presentWas the unresolved item visible, owned, and escalated in time?
Payer-requirement signalA relevant payer requirement may need verificationWas current authoritative payer guidance checked by the appropriate role?

Student Analysis

A downstream denial or rejection is a signal to investigate. It does not, by itself, prove which upstream condition caused the outcome.

Scope Boundary

DPIS does not make coding, medical-necessity, coverage, clinical, or payer-policy determinations. Those decisions remain outside this student project and require qualified review and current authoritative guidance.
