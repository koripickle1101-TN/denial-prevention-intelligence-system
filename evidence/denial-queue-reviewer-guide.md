## Reviewer snapshot

**Problem:** A downstream denial or claim-readiness signal may arise from unresolved earlier steps. **Approach:** Use five existing synthetic cases to practice documenting signal, evidence, routing, and a conservative status. **Evidence:** [Existing sample denial dataset](../data/sample-denial-cases.csv). **Relevant skills:** record review, hypothesis versus evidence, handoffs, status recording. **Limits:** These are not actual claims or verified payer denial root causes.
