Claims Attachment Expansion · CMS-0053-F

Requesting supporting information is a distinct workflow event.

CMS-0053-F adopts Version 6020 X12N 277 for a health care claim request for additional information and X12N 275 for additional information supporting a claim or encounter, together with adopted HL7 attachment standards. The final rule is limited to claims attachments; prior-authorization attachment standards were not finalized in that rule.

Important distinction: “277” is not enough context by itself. The adopted 5010 276/277 pair is used for claim-status inquiry/response, while CMS-0053-F adopts a Version 6020 X12N 277 specifically for a claims-attachment request for additional information. DPIS records the transaction purpose/version context rather than treating every “277” as the same operational event.
