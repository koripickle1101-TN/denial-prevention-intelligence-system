Denial-Risk Review Scorecard

A fictional scoring framework I use to practice organizing upstream workflow flags. The weights are educational design assumptions and are not validated denial probabilities, payer rules, or clinical/coding criteria.

Control Rule for the Simulation

Higher modeled scores trigger more student review or escalation in the fictional workflow. The score does not determine claim validity, coding accuracy, medical necessity, coverage, payment, or payer outcome.

Scope Boundary

Any coding-related, clinical, medical-necessity, coverage, or payer-policy question would require the appropriate qualified professional and current authoritative guidance. DPIS only models the presence of a review flag.
