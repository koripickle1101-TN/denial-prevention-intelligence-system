Payment Recovery Reconciliation Review™

A simulated revenue-cycle workflow for practicing what happens after money has moved: identify the financial signal, trace it to the original transaction, review available evidence, route unresolved work, reconcile the balance, and verify closure.

Student-developed · Simulated · Synthetic data only · No PHI

Operational Distinctions

Paid ≠ correctly reconciled.

Reduced payment ≠ explanation of the underlying recovery.

Adjusted balance ≠ reconciled account.

1. Identify the Financial Signal

2. Trace, Review, Route

Control Question

If a later payment is reduced because of an earlier account, what evidence should connect the recovery to the original transaction before the adjustment is treated as fully reconciled?

Patient-to-Professional Connection

The patient sees the balance. The operations team has to understand the chain of transactions that produced it.

If a recovery or adjustment is posted incorrectly or remains unresolved, a patient could experience a confusing balance, another statement, additional calls, or uncertainty about what they actually owe.

Career-Relevant Skills Practiced

This simulation practices recognizing a financial signal, tracing it to the original account, reviewing remittance evidence, separating facts from unanswered questions, routing ownership, documenting follow-up, and verifying closure.

Boundary

This project does not determine whether a payer, contract interpretation, code, refund, offset, recoupment, or reimbursement decision is legally or financially correct. Those determinations require the appropriate qualified role and current authoritative guidance.
